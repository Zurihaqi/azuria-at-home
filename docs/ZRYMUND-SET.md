# Zrymund's Astral Sovereign

Available in **Item Mall → AP → Gear Catalog → Zrymund's Sovereign Set**, 1 AP per piece.

The 11 pieces include Starbreaker Whip, Crown of Infinite Dawn, Eventide Aegis, Voidbinder Girdle, Hands of Eternity, Heavenstride Boots, Mantle of the First Star, Heart of the Cosmos, Seal of Absolute Dominion, Chronicle of Infinity, and Singularity Core.

All require level 1. The whip has 1,000,000 base DMG; the chest has 5,000,000 base HP. Other pieces have 200,000 base DMG and 2,000,000 base HP, alongside high critical, recovery, defense, and evasion values. Native game caps still apply to percentages.

The whip's two enchant definitions configure physical/magical life steal, critical damage, movement speed, damage dealt, damage reduction, penetration, and all elemental damage. Boots have an additional movement bonus. Actual runtime effects and client display require an equip test; definitions and native stat parsing have been verified.

The mall delivery enhancement hook upgrades eligible armor, weapons, necklaces, and rings through the build's normal fortification routine, up to +30. Five stars apply only to types and qualities supported by the native evolution command. Trophies and cloaks retain their native enhancement restrictions.

No character gear was replaced or forcibly equipped. Buy and test one piece first. To report a problem, include the item name, screenshot, and whether it happens while previewing, claiming, equipping, or entering a map.

Recovery sources are recorded in `zrymund-set-deployment.json`; the pre-custom-set backup contains the previous DBs, server item/enchant files, and original binary mask bytes. Client backups are under `private/client-original` with `.pre-zrymund-set` suffixes. Keep client and server definitions synchronized when restoring.
